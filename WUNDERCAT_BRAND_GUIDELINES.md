# Wundercat Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Architecture

This document is the single authority on how Wundercat is expressed visually and verbally. It applies to every piece of content across every channel.

### 1.1 The Wunderdog Brand Family

| Brand | Positioning | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| Wunderdog | Premium fresh cooked dog food. Consumer brand. | Health-conscious dog owners in the UAE |
| Wundercare | Therapeutic/clinical dog nutrition. Vet-prescribed. | Veterinary professionals and owners of ill dogs |
| Wundercat | Fresh gently cooked cat food. Purpose-built for feline biology. | Health-conscious cat owners in the UAE |

Wundercat is NOT a rebranded Wunderdog product. It is formulated specifically for the obligate carnivore metabolic profile of cats, with its own visual identity.

### 1.2 Shared Values

- Fresh, gently cooked: never raw, never ultra-processed
- 100% in-house production in Al Quoz, Dubai
- Formulated for nutrition, not cost
- Direct-to-consumer: no distributors, no retailers
- Locally sourced, Halal-certified ingredients
- British English throughout all communications
- AAFCO and FEDIAF compliant formulations

## 2. Brand Personality and Voice

### 2.1 Core Personality

A respected feline nutritionist who deeply understands both the science of cat nutrition and the bond between cats and their owners. Professional, warm, and quietly confident. Evidence-based in everything they say.

### 2.2 The Four Pillars

| Pillar | Application |
|---|---|
| Scientific authenticity | Lead with expertise and evidence. Precision matters: specific temperatures, specific nutrient data. Not vague claims. |
| Professional warmth | Clear, human language. Subtle humour. Never cold or clinical. Acknowledge the emotional weight of nutrition decisions. |
| Evidence-based accessibility | Present scientific concepts clearly. Always connect research to real-world observable benefits. Never leave a statistic unexplained. |
| Solution orientation | Actionable guidance, not lectures. Respect that cat owners are here to solve problems. |

### 2.3 Tone Calibration vs Wunderdog

Wundercat tone is warmer and more urgent than Wunderdog on appetite and health monitoring. Cats carry significantly higher medical risk from fasting (hepatic lipidosis risk within 48 hours in overweight cats). Where Wunderdog support can be patient and encouraging, Wundercat support must be warmer, faster, and more direct about when to seek veterinary advice.

### 2.4 Humour Standard

**Correct**
> "We test every recipe extensively. The slow-blink approval from the tasting panel is just a bonus."

**Incorrect**
> "Your fur baby will go purr-fectly crazy for these yummy noms!"

## 3. Logo and Wordmark

### 3.1 The Wundercat Wordmark

The Wundercat wordmark is set in Stacion font in Wundercat Pink (#C3648D). The wordmark is always presented in lowercase. Three variants:

- Pink wordmark on white or cream background (primary use)
- White wordmark on dark background (reversed)
- White wordmark on pink (#C3648D) background

### 3.2 Logo Rules

- Minimum size: 8cm in print (3cm for icon element alone)
- Clear space: 2cm minimum each side
- Never distort, rotate, recolour, add effects to, or crop the logo
- Never place on busy or dark photographs without sufficient contrast
- Never use smaller than minimum size under any circumstances

## 5. Typography System

Wundercat uses a three-font system aligned with the Wunderdog brand family, with role assignments adapted for the cat sub-brand.

### 5.1 Print and Packaging Typography

| Font | Role | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Stacion | Logo wordmark | EXCLUSIVELY for the wundercat logo. Never for body text or general headlines. |
| Info Story | Display and headlines | All hero headlines, section headers, callout text, flyer titles, and featured messaging. Used on all Wundercat print collateral. |
| Poppins | Body text | All body copy, captions, labels, supporting text, tables. Consistent with Wunderdog and Wundercare. |

### 5.2 Web and Digital Typography

Info Story is not available as a standard web font. For website and digital assets, use the following substitution system:

| Role | Print Font | Web/Digital Substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Logo | Stacion | Pre-rendered image or SVG (do not substitute) |
| Display headlines | Info Story | Fredoka (Google Font): closest match to Info Story's rounded, warm character. Free, multiple weights. |
| Body text | Poppins | Poppins (Google Font): no substitution needed. Same font across all channels. |

Alternative web substitutes for Info Story: Quicksand (more restrained, geometric) or Baloo 2 (more playful, rounder). Fredoka is the recommended first choice because it most closely matches Info Story's rounded letterforms and warm personality.

### 5.3 Sizing for Visual Assets

| Format | Headlines (Info Story) | Subheads | Body (Poppins) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stories (9:16) | 48px Bold | 32px Semi Bold | 28px Regular |
| Carousels (1:1) | 42px Bold | 28px Semi Bold | 24px Regular |
| Blog banners | 56 to 72px Bold | 32 to 48px Semi Bold | 20 to 32px Regular |

For AI-generated visuals (Nano Banana Pro), describe Info Story as a "rounded bold display sans-serif" and specify Poppins explicitly for body text.

## 6. Photography and Image Treatment

### 6.1 Always Use

- Full-bleed images filling the entire frame
- Deep-etched (cut-out) cats on solid brand colour backgrounds
- Photorealistic cats with breed variety: mixed breeds, Persians, Maine Coons, British Shorthairs, Siamese, tabbies, gingers, black cats
- Clean, minimal layouts with generous white space or warm cream space
- One clear focal point per design
- Simple line icons in brand pink for instructional content (as per Tips flyer style)

### 6.2 Never Use

- Boxed or framed images
- Gradients of any kind (solid colours only)
- Small decorative icons: stars, paw prints, hearts, sparkles, dots, or ornamental shapes
- Drop shadows, glows, or heavy visual effects
- Illustrated or cartoon cats (exception: the cat silhouette framing element established in Tips flyer)
- Dark or moody palettes
- Clip-art style graphics, 3D or glossy icon effects

### 6.3 Established Design Elements

The following design elements are established across the produced Wundercat collateral and should be used consistently:

- Cat silhouette as a structural framing element (die-cut or background shape)
- Rounded corner content cards on warm cream or blush pink backgrounds
- Simple line icons in brand pink (#C3648D) for instructional and tips content
- Teal (#4FC3CA) check marks for feature/benefit lists

## 7. Vocabulary Rules

These rules are hard requirements, not suggestions. They apply to every channel, format, and team member.

### 7.1 Use and Never Use

| USE | NEVER USE |
|---|---|
| "Cats", "your cat" | "Fur babies", "kitty-witty" |
| "Cat owner" or "pet parent" | "Cat mom", "cat dad" |
| "Recipe", "pâté" | "Noms", "yummies", "munchies" |
| "Fresh, gently cooked" | Just "fresh" when cooking is the distinction |
| "Nutrition" or "diet" | "Food" in therapeutic contexts |
| "Feline", "kitten" | "Doggo", "puppers" or any dog terms |
| "Evidence-based", "research-backed" | Unsupported superlatives: "best", "revolutionary" |

### 7.2 Absolute Prohibitions

- No em dashes under any circumstances (use colons, commas, or restructure)
- No cat puns or cat-related wordplay ("purr-fect", "meow-nificent")
- No baby talk or anthropomorphised cat voices
- No comparison to wild cat diets implying cats should eat raw
- Never describe Wundercat as "raw": always "fresh, gently cooked"
- "Pâté" spelled with both accents throughout
- British English throughout all communications

## 8. Tone of Voice

| Tension | How to Hold It |
|---|---|
| Professional yet warm | Knowledgeable but approachable. Never stiff or cold. Never casual or breezy. |
| Conversational but not casual | Direct speech. Never slip into informality that undercuts authority. |
| Authoritative without being academic | Cite science. Always translate into real-world language immediately after. |
| Empathetic without being sentimental | Acknowledge the emotional weight of nutrition decisions. Never melodramatic. |
