Cancellation expected 7 days before billing; inside window charge runs, month delivers, penalty applies. Unpaid penalty -> delinquent flag blocking re-subscription on verified phone+email. - Billing: anchor on signup day; charge 02:00 Dubai = 22:00 UTC previous day (day-offset, property-tested; fallback 04:00 Dubai). Payment reminder 7 days before every charge. Stripe billing_cycle_anchor_config, flexible mode, API 2025-06-30.basil+. pause_collection NEVER used; Portal card-update-only. Delivery gate: nothing ships except on invoice.paid; recovered payments rebook automatically.
- Failed payments: retry 7 days (support personal call), then inactive_non_payment 30 days (reactivatable, everything retained), then cancelled + penalty (commitment) + delinquent where uncollectable. Reactivation date = new billing anchor; ADVISORY pending ruling: commitment counts 12 paid months.

## Mobile app model rulings (August 2026, reconstructed 10 Sep 2026)
Reconstructed from the registry supersession log and the mobile-app document set after the skill reverted to an early-August snapshot. These are the August decisions that govern the mobile app; where they touch the early-August sections above, they update them as noted.

- **Prices and baskets (Pricing v5).** The mobile app works to Pricing v5, which supersedes the legacy indicative prices in Plans and money above. Two baskets, both VAT inclusive: Home Essentials Basket (6 products) one-time 204 / monthly 173 / 12-month 143; Complete Home Basket (7 products) one-time 283 / monthly 241 / 12-month 198. Savings baselines, each pill naming its own: one-time 7 percent vs buying products individually; monthly a further 15 percent and 12-month a further 30 percent vs the one-time basket price. GATE: Omer signs off the 12-month prices (143 and 198) before any subscription price is shown in-app or created as a Stripe Price, because they compute above the previously publicised 139 and 189. All three plans are live from the 20 Aug launch; the plan set is configuration (per the 4 Aug architecture ruling).
- **Sign-in and cart.** Browse free, sign in to buy: guests browse the shop and product detail, but adding to the cart or buying requires a phone plus one-time-code sign-in (S12), before checkout and never inside it. No guest cart. Pay then schedule: payment confirms the order, the slot is chosen after (S18) or skipped; a zone with no open slots still completes the order and the customer is contacted within one working day.
- **Recurring add-ons (ruling 21 Aug, IN SCOPE for launch).** A customer can add individual products to a subscription. The cart NEVER deletes items. Add to plan is ADDITIVE and independent of the quantity stepper: the stepper delivers the item in this order now, the chip ALSO subscribes it (recurring from the next refill), so a line can be now only, on the plan only, or both. Add-on pricing: the plan subscriber discount (15 percent monthly, 30 percent 12-month; a 12-month add-on sits under the 12-month commitment and its early-cancellation fee). Timing: from the plan payment (in the first plan payment at signup, else the next renewal, no mid-cycle proration). Refill-bottle add-ons scale the plan bottle count and join the AED 69 rule. Recurrence is managed in Manage plan (the Manage add-ons screen, S26); Change basket or plan is a separate action. OPEN: any per-plan cap; whether an add-on change counts against the two-changes-a-month budget; all-seven-products vs a subset.
- **Basket quantity.** All baskets can be bought in quantity, one-time and subscription alike, via a cart stepper. Quantity scales the price; for a subscription each refill delivers and is billed for the chosen quantity (for example two Home Essentials monthly is AED 346 a month, 2 x 173), and the bottle set and swap count scale (two Home Essentials is 12 bottles, shown 0 of 12). It stays one plan with a quantity, not two subscriptions. Quantity zero removes the basket. The bottle count must be data-driven.
- **Bottles, place-in-subscription labelling.** The subscription order set is kept; the first refill has no swap; the swap runs from the SECOND refill onward, which is the third delivery (physical timing unchanged from the Bottles section above). Customer-facing copy uses "from your second refill" so the screen and the message agree; "third delivery" is the same visit, numbered differently. AED 69 forgiven on the first two missed swaps, charged on the third. Counts scale with basket quantity.
- **Order-level cancel (mobile).** Distinct from cancelling a plan: an order can be cancelled from Order detail (S21) before it is packed, refunding the full amount to the card and issuing a credit note; once packed or out for delivery the action routes to support. OPEN: refund window and launch scope.
- **Free-delivery nudge.** The existing fee rule (free at AED 100 or more, otherwise AED 20, assessed before discounts) is surfaced in every cart or checkout where the deliver-now goods are under AED 100: "Add AED X more for free delivery", flipping to "Free delivery unlocked" at AED 100 or more. Display only, no rule change.
- **Back-in-stock (Scope Change Requirement D).** An out-of-stock product card offers Notify me; on restock the platform fires a back-in-stock notification (in-app plus channels). OPEN: consent basis for the outbound channels (marketing vs utility).
- **Vocabulary (August addition to the hard-stops above).** Reserve "refill" for subscriptions only. The generic order chrome (which also covers one-time orders and single products) is order-type-neutral: Home reads "Active orders" not "refills", and empty states invite shopping generally. "Refill" is kept for genuine subscription refills (refill plan, refill day, next refill, bottle swap). The "refill day", never-a-pause and cycle-for-billing-only rules above still stand.
